"The Tired Dark Lord" — a monologue of about 100 words spoken by Malachar, a fantasy villain who has razed nine realms and is secretly just very tired. He receives the hero Ser Alden for their dawn duel and keeps putting it off: a sagging step, a leaking tower, ravens on strike, a war council at an ungodly hour, a kettle just boiled. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the repeated words and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary — and the last line is the sting: one cup of tea first, then either of them may kill the other. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

It writes the monologue to `out/entry.txt` (532 characters, UTF-8, no trailing line end).
